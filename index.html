003 Case file · FRP-0212

It didn't fail at 2 a.m. It failed three weeks earlier.

Symptom

Overnight high-vibration trip

Boiler-feed pump P-104 trips on high vibration at 02:14. Operations resets, it trips again in 40 minutes. Production is down 6 hours.

4.1 mm/s and climbing
Root cause

0.4 mm of soft-foot misalignment

The vibration signature peaked at 2× running speed — the fingerprint of misalignment. A coupling check confirmed it. The bearing was a symptom, not the disease.

2× RPM dominant peak
Corrective action

Laser alignment + soft-foot correction

Shims under the inboard foot, alignment to 0.05 mm. Vibration dropped to 1.2 mm/s. The seal and bearing that were "about to fail" were never the problem.

1.2 mm/s after
